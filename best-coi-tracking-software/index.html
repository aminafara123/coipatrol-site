Best COI tracking software in 2026: an honest comparison with real prices

Updated November 2026. Written for US construction and property firms.

Most "best COI software" lists are written by a vendor that ranks itself first. We make CoiPatrol, so read this with that in mind. We have tried to be fair anyway. That includes saying where other tools beat ours.

What is the best COI tracking software for a small contractor?

It depends on your size. Under about 10 subs, a free spreadsheet or a free tier is enough. Between 10 and 1,000 subs, a self-serve tool with public pricing usually fits best. Past 1,000 vendors, or if you need carrier verification or a review team, an enterprise platform is worth its higher price.

How do the main COI tracking tools compare on price?

Most enterprise tools do not publish a price. You get a quote after a demo. Third-party estimates put entry plans at roughly $1,000 to $3,000 a year. Some tools charge a $7,500 or $10,000 minimum. Self-serve tools publish their prices, with free tiers and paid plans from $19 to $199 a month.

When should you pick an enterprise COI platform instead?

Pick an enterprise platform if you manage more than 1,000 vendors. Pick one if you need certificates verified directly with the insurance company. Pick one if you want a team of people to review every certificate for you. Those are real needs, and tools like myCOI, CertFocus and bcs full service meet them well.

Enterprise tools also suit firms where the insurance or risk department, not the office admin, owns the process. If that is you, the onboarding time and the price are worth it.

How does CoiPatrol compare with COI Watch and COI Guard?

What are CoiPatrol's limits?

CoiPatrol does not verify policies directly with insurance carriers. It does not include a managed team that reviews certificates for you. Its largest plan covers up to 1,000 subcontractors. What is a good myCOI alternative for a small company?

A good myCOI alternative for a small company publishes its price and lets you start without a sales call. Third-party estimates put myCOI near $2,000 a year to start. CoiPatrol is $49 a month for up to 50 subs, free up to 10. bcs self-service, COI Watch and YourStanding are also self-serve.

myCOI is now illumend, "from the team behind myCOI", according to illumend's about page. Our myCOI alternative guide compares illumend with self-serve tools in detail.

What is a good TrustLayer alternative?

TrustLayer's free plan for up to 50 vendors is generous, so try it first if you fit its form. If you want to skip the fit form, or you need a plan with a public price above the free tier, look at a self-serve tool. CoiPatrol, bcs self-service and YourStanding all publish their prices.

How much does COI tracking software cost?

Enterprise platforms like myCOI, Jones and Billy are quote-only and cost several hundred dollars a month. TrustLayer is free up to 50 vendors, then $10 per extra vendor a month. Small-business tools run from about $20 to $199 a month by vendor count. CoiPatrol is free for up to 10 subcontractors and $49 a month for up to 50.

Do I need COI software at all?

Not always. A spreadsheet works with a handful of subs if one person checks it every week. It breaks at the follow-up, because it cannot send renewal reminders. If you are chasing more than about 15 subs, or a lapsed policy has already slipped past you, software is cheaper than the gap.
