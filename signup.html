What you get on the free plan

  • Track up to 20 subcontractors, free, with no time limit and no credit card.
  • AI reading of every certificate, scans too.
  • A private upload link per sub, a green, yellow and red dashboard, CSV import and export, and certificates by email.
  • The weekly summary, certificate history and one login.
  • A one-click "Send reminder" button per sub.

Free is the door. Paid plans do the chasing for you: automatic renewal reminders, the email to the sub saying what to fix, requirements by trade, team members, the audit ZIP, your own reminder wording and schedule, and emails sent as your company.

For more than 20 subs, paid plans are $49 a month for up to 50, $99 a month for up to 250 and $199 a month for up to 1,000, each with a 14-day trial. A card is asked at checkout and nothing is charged for 14 days. The first month on a monthly plan is 10% off. See pricing.
