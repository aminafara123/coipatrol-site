myCOI alternative: what changed, and what a small firm should use instead

Updated November 2026. Written for US construction and property firms.

If you are looking for a myCOI alternative, start with the news. myCOI has become illumend. This page explains the change, compares illumend with CoiPatrol honestly, lists other options with public prices and gives you a checklist for switching. We make CoiPatrol, so weigh our view with that in mind.

What happened to myCOI?

myCOI is now illumend. The team behind myCOI rebuilt the product as an AI-native compliance platform called illumend, and myCOI customers are being moved over to it. The old mycoitracking.com address now forwards to illumend.ai. Existing myCOI customers can still log in while their accounts move.

illumend's about page calls it "the AI-native COI compliance platform, from the team behind myCOI" and says "myCOI customers are moving to illumend now." When we checked on November 3, 2026, the old address mycoitracking.com redirected to illumend.ai.

Where is illumend the better choice?

illumend is the better choice when you want certificates reviewed for you. Its AI reviews every certificate and endorsement at upload, it follows up with vendors and their brokers until gaps close, and it offers compliance experts you can talk to. It also connects to Procore, Viewpoint, CMiC and Yardi. Large vendor lists suit it well.

Those details come from illumend's about page and home page. The about page says the team has spent 16 years in third-party insurance compliance. CoiPatrol has none of those integrations and no review team. If endorsement review and managed follow-up are what you need, illumend does that and CoiPatrol does not.

What other myCOI alternatives are there?

Certificial tracks up to 5 suppliers free, with paid plans from $99 a month. Each fits a different size of business, so compare them at your vendor count.

How do I choose the right myCOI alternative?

Start with your size and who does the work. A few dozen subs and one office admin points to a self-serve tool with a public price. Hundreds of vendors, carrier verification or a team to review certificates for you points to an enterprise platform. Then price each option at twice your current vendor count.

When should you stay with illumend?

Stay if you manage hundreds of vendors, if your team relies on someone else reviewing endorsements, or if you need the Procore, Viewpoint, CMiC or Yardi connection. Moving would mean taking that work back in house. Stay too if your contract runs for another year and the price works for you. Switching mid-contract can mean paying for two tools.

How long does it take to switch COI tracking tools?

For a small firm, moving the data is the quick part. Export your vendor list and current certificates, import the list into the new tool and upload the certificates. The slow part is chasing fresh certificates from subs whose files are out of date. Keep the old tool until every renewal date is set in the new one.

Use this checklist to switch without a gap in your records.

  1. Ask your account team for an export of your vendor list and certificates before your contract ends.
  2. Import the vendor list into the new tool. CoiPatrol takes a CSV file.
  3. Upload the current certificates. Anything expired or missing goes on a re-request list.
  4. Send the re-requests. Our COI request email templates have the wording.
  5. Check each new certificate as it arrives. Our guide to verifying a certificate of insurance has a free checker.
  6. Turn on reminders in the new tool, then turn them off in the old one, so subs do not get two sets of emails.
  7. Keep the old export. Your premium auditor may ask about last year.

What else do people ask about myCOI alternatives?

The questions below cover free plans, per-vendor versus flat pricing, and whether tracking software can tell you a policy is still active. They apply to any tool you are weighing up against illumend. Work out the cost at your size today and at twice that size before you decide.

Is free COI tracking software good enough?

For a few subs, often yes. Free tiers usually cap the number of vendors, and some cap features too. Check that the free plan sends reminders to your subs, not only to you. If you might outgrow it, check what the next plan costs before you load every certificate into it.

Should COI software charge per vendor or a flat price?

Either can work. Per-vendor pricing looks cheap at first and grows with every sub you add. A flat price is easier to budget and does not punish you for keeping old subs on file. Work out the cost at your size today and at twice that size before you decide.

Does COI tracking software check that a policy is still active?

Most small-business tools do not. They read the certificate and track its dates, so a policy cancelled mid-term can still look valid. Some enterprise platforms, and tools that share live policy data from the insurer, can catch cancellations. If you need that, expect a higher price and a longer setup.
