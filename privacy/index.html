Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This page explains what data CoiPatrol collects, why we need it, and what we do with it. The short version: we keep only what the service needs to work, we do not track you, and we never sell your data.

Who is responsible

CoiPatrol is run by Al Amin Bashir Afara, an individual founder based in the United Arab Emirates. For any privacy question or request, email hello@coipatrol.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your company name, your email address and your password. We store the password only in hashed form, so we cannot read it.
  • Certificates you upload: the PDF files, plus the insurance details we extract from them, such as insured names, insurers, policy dates and coverage limits.
  • Subcontractor details you enter: the names, trades, emails and phone numbers you add for your subcontractors, so CoiPatrol can track their certificates and email them renewal reminders on your behalf.
  • Emails to us: if you write to hello@coipatrol.com, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on the website or in the app.

Why we use it

We use your data only to run CoiPatrol for you: to let you sign in, to read your certificates, to show you which ones are current or expiring, to send confirmation, password reset, summary and renewal reminder emails, and to answer your questions. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it to anyone.

How certificates are read

CoiPatrol uses AI to read the certificate documents you upload and pull out the key details. This is done only to provide the service to you. Automated reading can make mistakes, so please check important details against the original certificate.

Where your data is stored

Your account data, uploaded certificate PDFs and extracted insurance data are stored on CoiPatrol's own server infrastructure. Passwords are hashed and access to the app requires signing in.

Service providers we use

A few outside companies help us run CoiPatrol. They process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Brevo sends our transactional emails: account confirmations, password resets, summaries to you and renewal reminders to your subcontractors. It receives each recipient's email address and the content of those emails.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS for coipatrol.com and serves the app at app.coipatrol.com. Traffic to the app passes through its network.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this marketing website. Like any web host, it may log basic request details such as IP addresses.

Cookies

The app uses one kind of cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is needed for the app to work. There are no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies on the app or on this website.

How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you want your account and data deleted, email hello@coipatrol.com from the address on your account and we will delete it. There is no self-serve delete button yet, so email is the way to do it for now.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email hello@coipatrol.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, we will also email account holders.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests go to hello@coipatrol.com.
