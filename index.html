How CoiPatrol works

  1. Add your subs. Type them in or import a CSV, and each sub with no certificate on file gets a request email.
  2. Subs send their certificates. Each sub gets a private upload link with no account, or you forward the agent's email to your company's certificate address. The same link takes a W-9, license or agreement too.
  3. CoiPatrol reads and checks it. It reads the carrier, limits and dates, checks them against your requirements, tells the sub what to fix and holds only unreadable ones for you.
  4. Renewals are chased for you. Subs get reminders before a policy expires, and you're copied on the lapse reminder. If the sub does not answer, the insurance agent on the certificate hears from us next.

What CoiPatrol reads on a certificate

CoiPatrol reads these parts of an ACORD 25, the one-page certificate most agents issue. The companies are made up.

  1. 1
    Insured. The sub's name, used to file emailed certificates under the right sub.
  2. 2
    Insurer. The carrier, saved with each policy.
  3. 3
    Type and policy number. Checked against the policy types you require.
  4. 4
    Policy dates. The expiration sets the green, yellow or red color and the reminders.
  5. 5
    Limits. Compared with your minimums: "General liability aggregate is $1,000,000. You require $2,000,000."
  6. 6
    Additional insured and waiver of subrogation. Read as yes or no, and flagged if you require them.

Who it is for, and who it is not for

CoiPatrol fits general contractors, specialty contractors, property managers and landlords with a handful to 1,000 subcontractors or vendors, usually handled by one office person.

It's not for you if you manage more than 1,000 vendors, need certificates verified with the insurer, or want a team to review every certificate. Enterprise platforms do that well. See our comparison or the myCOI alternative guide.

A lapsed certificate can land on your own premium. Read how a workers' comp audit counts missing certificates.

Questions before you sign up

Do my subs need an account?

No. Each sub gets a private upload link. The sub or their insurance agent uploads the ACORD 25 PDF there. The sub doesn't log in or install anything.

What happens when I import my list?

Import a CSV with name, email and trade columns. Every sub with no certificate on file gets a request email with their private upload link.

What if it reads something wrong?

Every upload page shows what was read next to the PDF. Remove a wrong policy line and add the right one. If a date or a limit can't be read, the certificate is held for you to type it in.

What does it cost?

Free for up to 20 subcontractors. $49 a month for up to 50, $99 for up to 250 and $199 for up to 1,000. Paid plans start with 14 days free. See pricing. Free tools: COI checker and request emails.
