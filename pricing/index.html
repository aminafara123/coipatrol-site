What does it cost to track certificates by hand?

A spreadsheet is free. The time is not. Someone has to read every certificate, type in the dates, check the list each week and send the chasing emails. Missed renewals happen silently. At your workers' comp premium audit, a missing certificate for an uninsured sub can be added to your own premium. One missed certificate can cost more than a year of CoiPatrol.

Pricing questions

How much does COI tracking software cost?

Enterprise platforms like myCOI, Jones and Billy are quote-only and cost several hundred dollars a month. TrustLayer is free up to 50 vendors, then $10 per extra vendor a month. Small-business tools run from about $20 to $199 a month by vendor count. CoiPatrol is free for up to 10 subcontractors and $49 a month for up to 50.

Is Excel good enough for COI tracking?

For a handful of subs, yes: a sheet with expiry dates and conditional formatting does the job. It breaks down past a few dozen vendors because it cannot send renewal reminders, chase subs automatically, or flag coverage gaps, and missed renewals happen silently.

Do my subs need an account?

No. Each sub gets a private upload link. The sub or their insurance agent opens it and uploads the ACORD 25 PDF. No login, no password, nothing to install.

Can certificates come in by email?

Yes. Your company gets its own certificate address. Forward the agent's email there and CoiPatrol files the PDF under the matching sub. Anything it cannot match waits in an Unassigned queue for you to file.

What if it reads something wrong?

Every upload page shows what was read next to the PDF. Remove a wrong policy line and add the right one. If CoiPatrol cannot read a date or a limit, it files nothing and asks you to type it in.

Can I bring my list of subs?

Yes. Import a CSV with name, email and trade columns. Every sub with no certificate on file gets a request email automatically.

What counts toward my subcontractor limit?

Each active subcontractor or vendor counts once, no matter how many policies they have. Archive a sub when the job ends and they stop counting. You can bring them back later.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The Growth, Pro and Business plans start with 14 days free. You add a card when the trial starts. Free never needs one. The Free plan has no time limit for up to 10 subcontractors.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan never asks for one. Paid plans ask for a card when the trial starts, and nothing is charged until the 14 days end. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

What happens when the trial ends?

The card you entered is charged for the plan you picked, and nothing else changes. Cancel before the 14 days end and you pay nothing. Your account goes back to Free and keeps your subs and certificates. With more than 10 subs, you cannot add new ones until you archive some or upgrade.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month you cancel in. You can export all your data to a spreadsheet before you go.
