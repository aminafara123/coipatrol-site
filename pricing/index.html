What does it cost to track certificates by hand?

A spreadsheet is free, but the time isn't. Someone has to read every certificate, type in the dates, check the list each week and send the chasing emails. Missed renewals happen silently. At your workers' comp premium audit, a missing certificate for an uninsured sub can be added to your own premium. One missed certificate can cost more than a year of CoiPatrol.

Pricing questions

What does the Free plan leave out?

Free reads every certificate and holds up to 20 subs, with a one-click Send reminder button. It leaves out automatic reminders, the email telling a sub what to fix, requirements by trade, team members, the audit ZIP, your own reminder wording and schedule, and emails sent as your company.

How does the first month discount work?

Monthly plans get 10% off the first month, applied at checkout. It comes after the 14-day trial, and each company gets it once. Yearly billing has two months free instead. The 3 and 6 month options are at checkout.

How much does COI tracking software cost?

Enterprise platforms like myCOI, Jones and Billy are quote-only and cost several hundred dollars a month. TrustLayer is free up to 50 vendors, then $10 per extra vendor a month. Small-business tools run from about $20 to $199 a month by vendor count. CoiPatrol is free for up to 20 subcontractors and $49 a month for up to 50.

Is Excel good enough for COI tracking?

For a handful of subs, yes: a sheet with expiry dates and conditional formatting does the job. It breaks down past a few dozen vendors because it can't send renewal reminders, chase subs automatically, or flag coverage gaps, and missed renewals happen silently.

Can I group subs by project or job?

Yes. Put each sub on a project, then filter the dashboard by project. The CSV export and the audit ZIP filter by project too, and a projects page shows the sub count per project. It works on every plan, including Free.

Can subs send a W-9 through the link?

Yes. A sub can send a W-9, license, agreement or other document through the same private upload link, with no account. The files are stored privately and never read by any model. You can require a W-9 from every sub and see a "W-9 missing" tag. The CSV export shows who has one.

Does CoiPatrol work with Zapier?

Yes, with no Zapier app needed. Paid plans send signed JSON webhooks to one URL per company. Zapier, Make and n8n take that URL as a catch-hook. The eight events are certificate filed, needs review, held, policy expiring, policy expired, missing certificate, document received and sub added.

Is there an API?

Yes, on paid plans. Each company gets its own API keys. You can check status, list and read subs with their policies and certificates, download a certificate, create a sub and request a certificate. Limit is 60 requests a minute. The docs are public at app.coipatrol.com/api.

Can I check a certificate without signing up?

Yes. Upload a PDF, JPG or PNG on our verify page and CoiPatrol reads the insured, agent, carriers, limits and dates, then compares them to typical minimums. It is free, limited to three a day per visitor, and nothing is stored. You need no account.

Do my subs need an account?

No. Each sub gets a private upload link. The sub or their insurance agent opens it and uploads the ACORD 25 PDF. The sub doesn't log in or install anything.

Can certificates come in by email?

Yes. Your company gets its own certificate address. Forward the agent's email there and CoiPatrol files the PDF under the matching sub. Anything it can't match waits in an Unassigned queue for you to file.

What if it reads something wrong?

Every upload page shows what was read next to the PDF. Remove a wrong policy line and add the right one. If CoiPatrol can't read a date or a limit, it files nothing and asks you to type it in.

Can I bring my list of subs?

Yes. Import a CSV with name, email and trade columns. Every sub with no certificate on file gets a request email automatically.

What counts toward my subcontractor limit?

Each active subcontractor or vendor counts once, no matter how many policies they have. Free holds 20. Archive a sub when the job ends and they stop counting. You can bring them back later.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Growth, Pro and Business start with 14 days free. You add a card at the start, and nothing is charged for 14 days. The first month on a monthly plan is 10% off, applied at checkout. Free needs no card and has no time limit for up to 20 subcontractors.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No, not for Free. A paid trial asks for a card at checkout, and nothing is charged for 14 days. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

What happens when the trial ends?

The card you entered is charged for the plan you picked, and nothing else changes. Cancel before the 14 days end and you pay nothing. Your account goes back to Free and keeps your subs and certificates. With more than 20 subs, you can't add new ones until you archive some or upgrade.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month you cancel in. You can export all your data to a spreadsheet before you go.

What if I have more than 1,000 subcontractors?

Email us at hello@coipatrol.com and we'll talk about a larger plan. At that size an enterprise platform may suit you better. Our comparison lists them.
