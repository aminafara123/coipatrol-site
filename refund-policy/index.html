Refund Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

If you pay for CoiPatrol and you are not happy with it, you should not be stuck paying. This page explains how refunds and cancellations work.

The short version

If you are on a paid plan and unhappy for any reason, email hello@coipatrol.com within 30 days of a charge and we will give you a full refund of that charge. No questions asked, and no forms to fill in.

Which plans this covers

CoiPatrol has a free plan for up to 10 subcontractors, which never costs anything. The paid plans are $49 a month for up to 50 subcontractors and $99 a month for up to 150. Paid plans are not on sale yet. This policy applies to every charge once billing launches.

Paid plans are planned to start with a 14-day trial, so you can try CoiPatrol with your own certificates before you are charged at all.

How to ask for a refund

Send an email to hello@coipatrol.com from the address on your account, or tell us which account it is. Mention the charge you want refunded, for example the date and amount. You do not need to give a reason, although we are always glad to hear what we could do better.

We will confirm by email once the refund has been issued. The money goes back to the payment method you used. How long it takes to show up depends on your bank or card provider, and is usually a few business days.

The 30-day window

The 30 days are counted separately for each charge, starting on the date of that charge. For example, if you are charged on March 1, you can ask for that charge back until March 31. A refund covers the full amount of the charge you ask about.

Cancelling your plan

You can cancel a paid plan at any time. Cancelling stops all future charges. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again after that.

Cancelling and getting a refund are separate. If you cancel and also want your most recent charge back, and it was within the last 30 days, just say so in your email and we will do both.

Your data after you cancel

Cancelling a plan and deleting your account are also separate. If you want your account and certificates deleted, email hello@coipatrol.com and we will delete your account and data. See our privacy policy for details.

Contact

Questions about billing or refunds go to hello@coipatrol.com. This policy is part of our terms of service.
